Privacy Policy

Pretty Number Machine collects no data.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies, and no telemetry. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because the app does not contact any server once it has loaded. It works with no network connection at all.

The app stores a copy of its own files on your device so that it can run offline. That cache contains no personal information and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

No information is shared with anyone, because none is gathered.
